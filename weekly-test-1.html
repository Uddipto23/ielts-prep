Section 1 · Reading

Sixty minutes. Forty questions.

Three original passages that get harder as you go, a 60-minute countdown, and no feedback until you submit. Then your band estimate and an explanation for every answer.

Exam rules
  • 60 minutes in total for all three passages. There is no extra time to copy answers, so write straight into the answer boxes as you go.
  • Suggested split: about 17 minutes on Passage 1, 20 on Passage 2 and 23 on Passage 3. Passage 3 is the hardest, so protect its time.
  • Answers stay hidden until you press Submit. You can change any answer before then.
  • Spelling counts in gap-fill questions. Stay within the word limit given in the instructions, or the answer is wrong.
  • Never leave a blank. There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so guess.
  • Your place and answers are saved in this browser, so an accidental refresh will not lose the test.
