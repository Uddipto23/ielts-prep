Before you start

Four things that decide your band more than anything else in this plan.

Keep an error log

  • Write down every wrong answer
  • Note why: trap, vocabulary, misread, or time
  • Review the log weekly, not the correct answers
  • Patterns beat volume — 20 analysed mistakes > 200 rushed questions

Time yourself always

  • Reading: 20 min per passage, no exceptions
  • Writing: 20 min Task 1, 40 min Task 2
  • Listening: no pausing, no replays
  • Never practise untimed after week 1

Record your speaking

  • You cannot hear your own filler words live
  • Listen back for pauses, repetition, flat intonation
  • Shadow native audio to fix rhythm and stress
  • Two minutes daily beats two hours weekly

Know the band descriptors

  • Writing and Speaking are scored on 4 equal criteria
  • Reading/Listening band 7 ≈ 30 of 40 correct
  • Score your own work against official descriptors
  • Target your lowest criterion, not your favourite skill
