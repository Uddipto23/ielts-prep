The Day 2 routine · for every passage
  1. Title, then first sentences. Read only the title and the first sentence of each paragraph. Two minutes, no more.
  2. Label every paragraph in one word in the dashed boxes between paragraphs. That is your map.
  3. Headings first. Your labels are already most of the answer to matching headings.
  4. Then scan. For True / False / Not Given and gap-fill, use the map to jump to the right paragraph, then read that sentence closely. The map tells you where. It never tells you the answer.
